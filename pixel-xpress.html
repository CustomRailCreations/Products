Pixel Xpress Boxcar

An O gauge boxcar with a bright 2-inch color screen built into its side. Show your own railroad's logo, a family photo, an ad for your favorite layout business, or a slideshow of up to 7 pictures, and change them anytime from your phone.

Description

What it does

  • Color picture display on the side of the car, 320 × 240 pixels
  • One still picture, or a slideshow of up to 7, with your choice of time per picture
  • Pictures stay on the car when the power is off
  • Wireless setup and updates from the phone app, no cables
  • Buttons on the display: next/previous picture, pause and an info screen
  • Run several cars on one layout, each with its own pictures

Details

ScaleO gauge (1:48), 3-rail track
Screen2-inch color display
Pictures1 still, or a slideshow of up to 7
Wi-Fi2.4 GHz network (5 GHz-only networks won't work)
PhoneiPhone (iOS 16.4 or later) or Android, with Bluetooth
UpdatesInstalled from the phone app over Wi-Fi

Each car is custom built by hand, then loaded and tested. This is a collectible model for adult hobbyists, not a toy. It contains small parts and electronics and is not suitable for children under 14.

Usage

Setup takes about five minutes. You need 3-rail O gauge track, a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and a smartphone with Bluetooth.

Set up your car

  1. Get the appSearch for Pixel Xpress in your phone's app store. It's free.
  2. Power the carPlace it on powered track. The screen shows the Pixel Xpress logo.
  3. Connect itIn the app tap Scan Devices, pick your car and let the app join it to your home Wi-Fi.
  4. Add picturesChoose pictures from your phone, crop them to fit and set how long each one shows.

Everyday use

  • Change pictures any time from the app. The car remembers them.
  • Use the buttons on the display for next, previous, pause and info.
  • Press Y to see the software version and IP address.
  • Moving to a new Wi-Fi? Hold Y for 5 seconds, then add the car again in the app.
  • Install software updates from the app with Update Software.

Quick answers

Which Wi-Fi does it need?

A 2.4 GHz network. Most home routers have one. Networks that are 5 GHz only don't work.

How many pictures can I show?

Up to 7 in a slideshow, or a single still picture.

The display won't start.

Unplug the car for 10 seconds and try again. If it's still stuck, the Pixel Xpress Flasher repair tool reinstalls the software and keeps your pictures and Wi-Fi settings.

The app can't find my car.

Make sure the car is on powered track and your phone's Bluetooth is on, then scan again. See the troubleshooting guide for more.
