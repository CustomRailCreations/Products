Description

What it does

  • Color picture display on the side of an O gauge boxcar
  • Manage pictures over USB, with no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth involved
  • Play animations such as the ICBM Launch download
  • Switch back to your pictures any time
  • Reinstall or update the car's software from the desktop app

Details

ScaleO gauge (1:48), 3-rail track
Screen2.0-inch or 1.14-inch color display, depending on the car
ConnectionUSB cable to a Windows PC or Mac
SoftwareFree Pixel Xpress desktop app

Each car is custom built by hand, then loaded and tested. This is a collectible model for adult hobbyists, not a toy. It contains small parts and electronics and is not suitable for children under 14.

Usage

  1. Install the desktop appDownload the free Pixel Xpress desktop app on your Windows PC or Mac.
  2. Connect the carPlug the car into your computer with a USB cable.
  3. Manage contentUse the app to send pictures and install or play animations (Settings > Animations).
  4. Run it on the layoutPlace the car on powered track. It keeps showing what you loaded.
